Questions

Is it free?

Every device gets 2 free games each day. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited games – there's no subscription. The Android version also shows a small ad banner on the start screen.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.6 or later (coming soon to the App Store), and Android devices with Android 8.0 or later (on Google Play).

Does it need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Internet is only needed for purchases, and on Android for the ad banner.

What data does the app collect?

On iPhone and iPad: none. There are no accounts, and progress is saved only on the device. On Android, Google's ad service receives technical data, such as the IP address, to show ads that are never personalized. Read the app's privacy policy.

Can my child buy things by accident?

Every purchase and restore first shows a parental gate with a question for a grown-up, and it can't be turned off. Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy, and Google Play's parental controls, work too.
