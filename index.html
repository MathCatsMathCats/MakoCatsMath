All four operations

Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division – or a mix. Start with numbers up to 10 and grow all the way to 100.

Questions

Is it free?

Your child can play 2 free games every day. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited games and every cat. There's no subscription.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.6 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Internet is only needed to buy or restore the full version.

What data does the app collect?

None. There are no accounts, and progress is saved only on the device. Read the app's privacy policy.

Can my child buy things by accident?

Every purchase and restore first shows a parental gate with a question for a grown-up, and it can't be turned off. Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy work too.
