Purchases

How many games are free?

2 games every day, with every operation, number range and answer mode. The count starts again the next day.

What does the full version include?

Unlimited games and every cat to unlock. It's a one-time purchase – no subscription. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store in your currency.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Then, in the app, open the unlock screen and tap Already purchased? Restore. You can reach it from the settings button on the start screen: For Parents: Unlock Full App.

A grown-up question appears first – that's the parental gate.

What is the parental gate?

Before every purchase, every restore and every link that leaves the app, Mako Cats Math asks a question meant for a grown-up. It can't be turned off. You can also manage purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

The purchase didn't work or I'd like a refund

Payments are handled by Apple, so we can't see or change them. First try Already purchased? Restore, as described above. If that doesn't help, check that the device is online and signed in to the App Store.

Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Playing

What can my child practice?

Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, on their own or mixed, in four number ranges:

  • Beginner: 1–10
  • Easy: 1–50
  • Intermediate: 10–50
  • Advanced: 10–100

Each game has 20 questions.

What are Pick, Type and Draw?
  • Pick: tap one of four big answers.
  • Type: enter the number on a number pad. After a wrong answer there's one more try.
  • Draw: write the number with a finger. It's read on the device, without the internet.

Choose one under "How to answer" before each game.

Draw mode doesn't read my child's number

Write the digits large and side by side, then tap ✓. The eraser clears the pad. If drawing is still tricky, the small row of digits under the pad types the number instead.

How are new cats unlocked?

With a perfect game – every answer right. Each number range has its own cats to discover. Cats still waiting to be found show as silhouettes in the collection.

How do I turn off the music or sounds?

Tap the settings button on the start screen. Music and sound effects each have their own switch.

How do I change the language?

In the same settings, pick English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Polski, Русский or 中文.

Privacy & data

What data does the app collect?

None. There are no accounts and no ads, and nothing is sent to us. Progress, gems, cats and settings are saved only on the device. The details are in the app's privacy policy.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring the full version needs a connection.

Can progress move to another device?

Progress isn't synced between devices, because it never leaves the device. It can come back when a whole device is restored from an iCloud or computer backup. The full-version purchase can always be restored (see above).

How do I delete my child's progress?

Delete the app. All its data is removed with it. If you install it again, restore the purchase to get the full version back.
